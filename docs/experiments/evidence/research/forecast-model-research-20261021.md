# Forecast model research, 2026-10-21

No inspected source establishes that another model improves net returns on Wallex. The strongest immediate comparison is **direct Kronos-small forecasting against our existing Kronos hidden-state plus Ridge head**, followed by **Chronos-2 with past-only covariates**. Newer finance-specific KiT and EXAONE deserve controlled experiments, but their reported results do not justify replacing the current bank without a fresh comparison.

This review used official repositories, model cards and papers. No package, model weight or large dataset was downloaded. A read-only hardware query found an NVIDIA RTX 3050 Laptop GPU with 6,144 MiB VRAM. Memory estimates below are weight-only arithmetic, not measured runtime or guaranteed fit.

## What the current system actually predicts

Our [Kronos adapter](../../../../wallex_lab/kronos.py) encodes 64 completed OHLCV bars using genuine frozen Kronos weights, then applies a TRAIN-fitted StandardScaler, PCA and Ridge regression to predict a three-bar close return. It does **not** use the autoregressive future-candle generator. The fitted head gives a point estimate, with no native forecast quantiles. The [inference bank](../../../../wallex_lab/model_signals.py) reproduces that head from its saved parameters and separately runs the actual fitted HGB and GRU.

The official Kronos API instead generates future OHLCV/amount paths, with sampling controls and path averaging. Small has 24.7M parameters and base 102.3M, both with 512-bar context; public weights and code are MIT. Thus direct generation tests a materially different use of the same financial pretrained model. [Official Kronos repository](https://github.com/shiyu-coder/Kronos)

Kronos reports financial forecasting gains, including Binance spot pairs, with pretraining through June 2024 and evaluation starting July 2024. That is more relevant than an electricity or retail leaderboard, but it still does not establish performance for three-bar TMN spot returns after Wallex costs. The paper's headline RankIC improvement is a ranking metric, not a return percentage. [Kronos paper, experimental protocol](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.02739v1)

EXAONE's model card reports first place across three FinVerse tiers: point accuracy, cross-sectional information coefficients, and portfolios. These are **author-reported financial benchmark results**, not independent reproduction. Synthetic pretraining reduces the obvious route of copying historical prices, but does not remove benchmark-selection or distribution-transfer risk. Its release also explicitly says each series is processed as an individual channel. [Official EXAONE model card](https://huggingface.co/LG-AI-Research/EXAONE-Forecast-for-Finance-1.0)

## KiT requires an evidence audit before promotion

KiT is genuinely relevant: it forecasts OHLCV paths and reports improvements over direct Kronos and general foundation models. Its paper reports mean return RankIC 0.0571 versus 0.0521 for fine-tuned Kronos-base, and mean volatility RankIC 0.6600. However, Appendix A.2 says the fine-tuned Kronos comparator checkpoint was chosen for its best test-set scores. That makes the comparator protocol unsuitable as a clean template. The paper specifies TRAIN through 2023 and VALIDATION during 2024–2025, while the released model card lists a 2025-12-31 training cutoff. These descriptions need reconciliation. [KiT paper, sections 4.1–4.2 and appendix A.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.34507v1)

The official card distinguishes the three-market `step_market_284M` bundle from `step_market_284M_train260410`, which is A-share-only and actually trained through 2026-03-09. Its filename does not mean April 10 training. Matching instrument IDs and normalization statistics are mandatory. The card says metadata was reconstructed and original training JSON hashes were not recorded. Do not invent IDs for Wallex pairs or refit normalization on TEST to make inference work. [Official checkpoint provenance](https://huggingface.co/Lucifer744/KiT_model)

At 15 minutes the public inference interface uses 800 historical bars and 64 future bars, versus our 64/3 task. A three-bar comparison would need a documented first-three-path readout and substantially longer history. The repository's historical A-share backtest also uses market rules and costs that must not be transplanted to Wallex. [Official inference requirements](https://github.com/Luciferbobo/KiT)

## A comparison that could establish improvement

This is a proposed local protocol, not a result from the cited models:

1. Preserve identical completed M15 bars, three-bar targets, gap exclusions and next-open execution across candidates. Include zero-return/random-walk, fitted HGB, GRU and current Kronos-head baselines.
2. Report return MAE/RMSE and sign accuracy alongside absolute-return/volatility error. For probabilistic models report pinball loss, interval coverage and width. Price-level accuracy alone can reward copying the latest price.
3. Use earlier windows to choose normalization, context, sampling budget and entry thresholds; purge horizon overlaps. Keep the next chronological period untouched. Previous exposed TEST results remain exploratory evidence, not a newly independent holdout.
4. Compute each model's **gross** three-bar return before applying the same current fee/slippage assumptions. Preserve path samples where available. A quantile interval or fraction of positive paths is not automatically a calibrated probability of a profitable fill.
5. Keep OHLCV, USDT/TMN and other observed covariates past-only. Future volume, future high/low, or later news cannot be supplied as known-future features. Calendar features alone may be known ahead. Benchmark close-only and OHLCV/covariate variants separately.
6. Save model/code revisions, all weight/config/statistics hashes, availability cutoffs, symbol mappings, inference seeds, p50/p95 latency and peak VRAM. Run one GPU model at a time. Distinguish a model's pretraining cutoff from the time our fitted head became available.

A 2026 study of financial return forecasting likewise finds that pretrained forecasters are useful priors but do not establish universal, reliable alpha. Its five-U.S.-equity setting is additional caution, not direct evidence about Wallex. [Primary financial forecasting study](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.27100v1)
