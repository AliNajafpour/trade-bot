# Short-history barrier survival model

The user approved the architecture in chat and explicitly requested implementation, training, calibration and temporal evaluation. This phase uses only the short-history candle branch, not external news, order books, gold or a trading executor.

Input: symbol, UTC minute-boundary decision time, explicit entry price, arbitrary TP/SL as prices or fractions, and completed native M1 OHLCV. Long and short orientations are supported mathematically; this does not establish short-trading availability. The output is TP-first, SL-first and no-touch at 5/15/60/240/1440 minutes plus TP conditional on a touch. Horizon expiry never closes a trade.

Use frozen April 24 to July 23 2026 Wallex M1 sources, verifying their recorded hashes. Last completed close is an explicitly disclosed historical entry proxy, not evidence of executable fills. Keep Trade-Bot read-only. Exclude gold with unknown clock/quote provenance. All source OHLCV must pass shared validation; gaps are never interpolated.

Sample origins every 30 minutes and generate fixed, volatility-scaled asymmetric barrier pairs using history only. One origin owns all its queries and split membership. Label the first future touched minute. When both levels touch within one minute and the opening price resolves neither, retain unknown cause; a gap or source end right-censors at the last contiguous minute. Store observation duration and censor reason. Train a five-bin conditional competing-risk likelihood, using the sum of TP and SL mass for ambiguous causes and only completed survival bins for censored rows.

Encode up to six hours of history in intraday scales 1/5/15 minutes, with convolutional patch embeddings, a temporal Transformer and barrier-conditioned cross-attention. Compare 180 and 360 minute lookback using validation only. Three independent seeds form each evaluated ensemble. A query-only geometry baseline is trained by the same likelihood. Shared preprocessing uses only each origin's history. No pretrained weights are used.

Two expanding chronological folds share calendar boundaries across assets. A full 24h purge protects fit/selection/calibration/test boundaries even for earlier events. Fold 1 fits through May 30, selects through June 7, calibrates through June 15 and tests through June 26. Fold 2 fits through June 26, selects through July 3, calibrates through July 9 and tests through July 23. Global architecture choice sees only fold-1 selection data. Early stopping uses selection only. Hazard temperature calibration uses calibration only. Tests are opened once after choices freeze. Historical periods were used by earlier project experiments: these are held-out evaluations for this pipeline, not project-wide untouched or prospective data.

Report masked three-class log loss, Brier score, classwise reliability, ambiguity/censor coverage, partial-label likelihood, symbol/geometry slices and paired daily-block bootstrap differences to baseline. Calibration preserves horizon coherence. Do not claim profitable execution or universal barrier monotonicity. Predict requests return support flags, model/data hashes, history cutoff and explicit price-touch semantics.
